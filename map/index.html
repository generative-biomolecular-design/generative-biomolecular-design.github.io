About the collection

The space of viable, functional biomolecules is vast and only sparsely explored. Rational design, directed evolution, and high-throughput screening have delivered many successful molecules, yet a new target or function may offer no obvious starting point. Generative models learn from observed biological data to propose candidates beyond a finite dataset.

This map accompanies Probing the Biomolecular Design Space with Generative Modeling. Following the review’s algorithmic perspective, it connects the foundations of generative modeling to de novo and controllable biomolecular design. Explore how different generators and control strategies navigate the design space across proteins, peptides, nucleic acids, small molecules, and cell states.
